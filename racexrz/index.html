Gameplay

Everything trying to kill you

Dodge, jump, boost, and survive. Each run is faster than the last.

🚗
Traffic & Trucks
Switch lanes to dodge rival cars and heavy trucks barrelling down the road.
⚡
Jump & Gaps
Leap over road gaps, rocks, bombs, and oil slicks before they end your run.
🔥
Nitro Boosts
Three nitro charges auto-refill over time. Use them wisely when pressure peaks.
🪙
Coin Magnet
Collect coins on every run. Get close enough and they pull toward your car.
💥
Near Miss Streak
Thread the gap between enemy cars to score near misses and trigger a 2× multiplier.
👾
Boss Encounters
Survive long enough and a boss will breach the road. Defeat it to keep racing.

Boss Fights

Something wicked this way comes

Each time you push past a new difficulty threshold, a boss intercepts the road. The music shifts. The road clears. Survive the encounter and earn a rank bonus — or end your run trying.
