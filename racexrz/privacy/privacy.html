Race XRZ 1000

PRIVACY POLICY · DLA SOFTWARE

Last updated: May 31, 2026

The short version: you can play Race XRZ 1000 without creating an account. Your chosen pilot name and local leaderboard records stay on your device. Limited gameplay metrics may be processed through Firebase. The free version uses Google AdMob for ads, and Google Play handles the optional Remove Ads purchase. Firebase Analytics is optional and enabled only if you opt in. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information Stored by Race XRZ 1000

Race XRZ 1000 does not require an account. Your chosen pilot name, race results, high scores, game settings, tutorial status, ads-removal status, age-gate status, terms-acceptance status, and Analytics choice are stored locally on your device.

We do not receive your full locally stored race history or your locally stored pilot name. Eligible local game data may be included in Android backup or device-transfer services depending on your device settings. The app-managed age-gate, terms-acceptance, Analytics-choice, ads-removal, tutorial, and games-played preferences are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Google services may manage their own data under Google's policies.

2. Advertising and Privacy Choices

The free version uses Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements. AdMob and its partners may process IP address, general location estimated from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, the Android Advertising ID where available, app-set ID, and other device, account, or usage identifiers under Google policies and your available choices.

Race XRZ 1000 uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising privacy choices where configured or required. When available, you can review or change those choices from Options → Privacy Choices.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Firebase Analytics is optional and disabled by default. If you opt in, it collects usage events such as game sessions, difficulty tiers, score milestones, language preferences, and in-app interactions. These events do not include your pilot name or email address. You can change this choice later in the Options menu.

Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports to identify and fix technical problems. Reports may include an installation identifier, device and app metadata, stack traces, relevant application state, Analytics breadcrumbs where available, and limited gameplay context such as your last score, rank, difficulty tier, and ads-removal status. We do not attach your pilot name or email address to these reports. Google processes and retains Firebase data under its applicable policies.

4. Google Play Billing

Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. We do not receive or store payment-card or bank-account details.

5. Permissions and Network Access

Race XRZ 1000 requests internet and network-state access for ads, privacy messaging, and Google Play services. It declares Android Advertising ID access for AdMob. It does not request precise location access.

6. Optional Score Sharing

If you choose to share a score card, Race XRZ 1000 creates an image in temporary app cache that may include your chosen pilot name and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you select a destination.

7. Children and Age Requirement

Race XRZ 1000 is intended for players aged 13 or older. It includes an age gate and blocks access when a player declares that they are under 13.

8. Your Choices and Data Retention

You can clear local race records from the Options menu. You can manage advertising privacy choices through Privacy Choices where available and through your device settings. Firebase Analytics is optional and can be enabled or disabled at any time from the Options menu. Uninstalling removes local app data, subject to eligible Android backups. Google handles third-party data retention and deletion under its applicable policies.

9. Security and International Processing

We use platform protections available to a mobile application, including encrypted on-device storage for sensitive preferences. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Race XRZ 1000, its services, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new update date.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete processed information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. For privacy requests, contact us.
