Med Time — Medication Reminder App

Account & data deletion request

Med Time accounts are created and signed in through Google Sign-In, using Firebase Authentication. We don't have a self-service "delete" button yet, so account deletion requests are handled manually by the developer.

Use the form below to send a deletion request. It will open your email app with a pre-filled message — just hit send.

Request Deletion

This should be the Google account email you use to log in to the app.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account — your Firebase Authentication record (linked to your Google email) will be deleted.
  • Medication & reminder data — this is stored locally on your device only. Deleting your account does not erase it from your phone; uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings to remove it locally.
  • Analytics data — aggregated, non-identifying usage data held in Firebase Analytics is not tied to your name and is retained per Google's standard retention period.
Requests are typically processed within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted. If you have questions, contact danielluz.alves@outlook.com directly.
