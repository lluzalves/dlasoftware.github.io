Privacy Policy
Your puzzle progress, medals and settings stay on your device. TessArea has no account system and never uploads your game content. We use pseudonymous analytics and crash reports to improve the game, and the free version shows ads served by Google AdMob; a one-time purchase removes the ads. 1. Who we are

TessArea is a puzzle game developed by Daniel Luz. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. Questions are welcome at .

2. Data that stays on your device

Your campaign progress, medals, unlocked levels, seen-tutorial flags, language choice and sound settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers for this data and cannot see it.

Android's automatic backup may copy these settings to your own Google account backup so they survive a phone change. That backup belongs to you and is governed by your Google account settings, not by us.

3. Data processed by Google services

TessArea does not require an account and does not upload your game content. Two Google services process data so the app can work:

Google Play Billing handles purchases. We receive the purchase state (for example, whether "Remove Ads" is owned) but never your payment details.

Google AdMob serves ads in the free version and may process device information such as advertising identifiers and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics receive pseudonymous usage events (for example, that a level was completed or a tutorial step was skipped) and crash reports with technical device data. These help us find bugs and balance the game. They contain no account identity — TessArea has no accounts — and we never include personal content in them.

4. Ads and your choices

The free version of TessArea shows ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change that choice at any time from the app's settings.

Purchasing Remove Ads permanently turns all advertising off.

5. Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles payment, receipts and refunds under the Google Play terms; we never see or store your card information. The purchase can be restored after a reinstall.

6. Children

TessArea is intended for players who are at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country, whichever is higher.

7. Your rights

Because your game data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes it. You can withdraw or change ad consent in the app, and manage ad personalization in your device's Google settings. For anything else, contact us.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date above.

Seu progresso, medalhas e configurações ficam no seu aparelho. O TessArea não tem sistema de conta e nunca envia seu conteúdo de jogo para servidores.
